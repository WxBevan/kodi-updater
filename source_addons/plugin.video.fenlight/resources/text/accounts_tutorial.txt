ACCOUNTS SETUP TUTORIAL
This guide will show you how to set up TorBox and Trakt inside Kodi.



TORBOX SETUP
TorBox is required for playback.

Go to this website:
https://torbox.app/subscription

Create an account and purchase a subscription.
At the bottom of the subscription page, enter this referral code for bonus days:

209bc076-4e6b-4352-91e3-070276e5aa76

You can also use this referral link directly:
https://torbox.app/subscription?referral=209bc076-4e6b-4352-91e3-070276e5aa76

Once your account is created, go to:

https://torbox.app/dashboard

Click your profile icon in the top-right corner.
Click Settings.
On the settings page, you will see your API Key.
Copy the API key exactly.

Go back to Kodi and open:
Accounts > TorBox Authorisation
Enter your TorBox API key.

Make sure the key is entered exactly, with no missing characters or extra spaces.



TRAKT SETUP
Trakt is used to sync your watch history, progress, and lists.

Go to this website:
https://trakt.tv/

Create a free Trakt account.
Once your Trakt account is ready, go back to Kodi.

Open:
Accounts -> Authenticate Trakt

Kodi will show a QR code or activation code.
Scan the QR code with your phone and sign in to your Trakt account.
Approve the connection.

Once Trakt is authenticated, return to Kodi.



FINISHED
After TorBox and Trakt are both set up, movies are ready to use.
Click Reload on the home page to refresh.
